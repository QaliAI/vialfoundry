# 00 — Executive Summary

**Project:** Apex Research Peptides (working name) — premium research-use peptide catalog / ecommerce-adjacent platform.
**Prepared:** June 2026
**Purpose:** Business strategy, positioning, payment-readiness, and marketing planning.

> **Read this first.** This dossier separates four things deliberately: **[FACT]** (verifiable from public sources), **[OBSERVED]** (competitor behavior we can see on public sites), **[INFERENCE]** (our reasoning, labeled as such), and **[REQUIRES COUNSEL]** (legal/regulatory questions only an attorney can resolve). Nothing here is legal advice. Do not treat any "research use only" structure as legally safe without attorney review.

---

## The one-paragraph version

The online research-peptide market is large, fast-growing, and full of money — and it is in the middle of the most aggressive U.S. regulatory crackdown the category has ever seen. In late 2024 through 2026 the FDA issued dozens of warning letters and several established vendors shut down or were raided. The opportunity is real because demand is enormous and most competitors look amateurish, make reckless human-use claims, and are one enforcement letter away from collapse. The risk is equally real: the exact behaviors that drive competitors' short-term revenue (weight-loss copy, GLP-1 comparisons, dosing guidance, selling injection supplies) are precisely what triggers enforcement and payment-processor termination. **Our edge is to be the cleanest, most documentation-first, most premium-looking operator in a sea of grey-market stores — and to treat payment processing and copy discipline as the core constraints, not afterthoughts.**

## What the market looks like

- **[FACT]** U.S. peptide-related search volume was reported at ~10.1M searches/month by January 2026, with roughly 60% weight-loss focused (semaglutide/tirzepatide dominate). Source: Lantern Sol / NexaMed SEO analyses.
- **[OBSERVED]** The market spans research-peptide sellers, research-chemical/SARM shops, lab/reagent suppliers, compounding/telehealth-adjacent operators, affiliate/review sites, and a large creator ecosystem (YouTube, Reddit, biohacking newsletters).
- **[OBSERVED]** Independent "vendor rating" sites (Finnrick, Best Peptide Brands, Outliyr, PeptidePick) function as both review hubs and affiliate funnels.

## Who the major online competitors are

Core Peptides, SwissChems, Cosmic Peptides, Lone Star Peptide Co., Biotech Peptides, Amino Asylum, ChemYo, PureRawz, Limitless Biotech (formerly Limitless Life Nootropics), Sports Technology Labs, Bulk Peptide Supply, plus warning-lettered/shut-down cautionary cases: Peptide Sciences (voluntarily shut down March 2026), USApeptide.com, Summit Research, Prime Peptides, Xcel Peptides. See `02_competitor_database.csv` and `03_competitor_deep_dive.md`.

## What the best competitors do well

- Batch-specific COAs with **HPLC + mass-spec** displayed on or near product pages (Cosmic Peptides, Sports Technology Labs).
- **Third-party testing guarantees** (e.g., refund-if-it-fails policies).
- Clean, fast, premium storefronts; same-day shipping signals; clear purity numbers (≥99%).
- Email capture + seasonal promos; affiliate/referral networks with dashboards and coupon codes.

## What risky competitors do wrong (and we must avoid)

- **[FACT]** Plastering "research use only" while describing appetite suppression, weight loss, glucose regulation, muscle growth — and selling bacteriostatic water/syringes in the same cart. FDA has stated the RUO disclaimer is a "ruse" when other evidence implies human consumption. (FDA warning letters; HealthLaw Alliance analysis.)
- GLP-1 brand comparisons ("same active ingredient as…"), testimonials implying human outcomes, dosing/reconstitution guides.

## Products & pricing (high level)

- **Highest-volume / highest-risk:** GLP-1-adjacent (semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide). These dominate demand **and** enforcement. **[INFERENCE]** Treat as avoid/Phase-later for our brand.
- **High-demand, comparatively lower public-claims risk if described as catalog records:** tissue/cell-signaling peptides (BPC-157, TB-500), GH secretagogues (CJC-1295, Ipamorelin), cosmetic/copper peptides (GHK-Cu).
- **[OBSERVED]** Example street pricing: BPC-157 5mg ~$39–49; TB-500 5mg ~$42–52; GHK-Cu 50mg ~$49–59 (Core Peptides). See `04_product_catalog_and_pricing.md`.

## Payment & banking constraints (the real bottleneck)

- **[FACT]** Mainstream processors (Stripe, PayPal, Square, Shopify Payments, WooPayments) decline/terminate this category; Mastercard's BRAM program specifically scrutinizes it.
- **[OBSERVED]** A specialized high-risk ecosystem serves the category: AllayPay, Corepay, Easy Pay Direct, Instabill, PayBlox, Vector Payments, Durango, PaymentCloud, PayKings, eMerchantBroker, plus ACH/eCheck options. Rolling reserves (~10–20%, 90–180 days) are common. See `08_payment_processor_and_banking_report.md`.
- **[INFERENCE]** Payment approval — not website quality — is the binding constraint on going live. Build everything else now; do not integrate live checkout until an approved processor is signed.

## Viable vs risky marketing channels

- **Viable:** SEO/content (compound-name + research-context pages), documentation-as-content (COA/testing methodology), B2B/researcher outreach, email, carefully-controlled affiliate/referral, review-site relationships.
- **Risky:** Paid ads (routinely banned), influencer human-use claims, GLP-1/weight-loss content, anything implying personal outcomes. See `06`, `07`, `12`.

## What we can do now / what to avoid

- **Do now:** Premium MVP, technical catalog, COA/batch presentation, controlled inquiry/access flow, email automation, analytics readiness, SEO foundation, processor outreach prep.
- **Avoid now:** Live checkout, GLP-1 marketing, human-use copy, injection supplies, dosing content, unverified claims.
