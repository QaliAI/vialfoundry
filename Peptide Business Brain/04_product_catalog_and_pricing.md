# 04 — Product Catalog & Pricing Analysis

*Market-wide view. **No dosage, use, reconstitution, or injection guidance appears here by design.** Product names are catalog identifiers only. Labels: [FACT]/[OBSERVED]/[INFERENCE]/[REQUIRES COUNSEL]. Phase tags reflect our regulatory-optics judgment, not legal clearance.*

> **Naming principle for our site:** describe every item as a **technical product record** (compound identifier, CAS where applicable, molecular formula, purity %, vial size, COA/batch reference, storage form). Do not describe what a compound "does" for a person.

---

## Category 1 — GLP-1-adjacent research peptides
- **Examples [OBSERVED]:** semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide.
- **Sold by:** Core Peptides, SwissChems, Summit Research, Prime Peptides, Xcel Peptides, many others.
- **Pricing [OBSERVED]:** Semaglutide 5mg ~$89–109 (Core Peptides); varies widely.
- **COA/testing expectation:** Buyers expect HPLC + MS; identity confirmation is critical.
- **Regulatory/payment risk:** **HIGHEST.** This is the epicenter of FDA warning letters (Dec 2024, Feb 2025, 50+ in Sept 2025) and Novo Nordisk/FTC pressure. Also the hardest category for payment approval.
- **Phase recommendation:** **AVOID for Apex (at minimum Phase-later, pending counsel).** **[INFERENCE]** The demand is huge but the enforcement and payment risk is disqualifying for a brand whose entire edge is being the clean operator.
- **Safer naming:** N/A — we recommend exclusion. **Risky naming:** any GLP-1 brand comparison ("same as Ozempic/Wegovy/Mounjaro").

## Category 2 — Growth hormone secretagogues
- **Examples [OBSERVED]:** CJC-1295, Ipamorelin, GHRP-6, Sermorelin, Hexarelin.
- **Sold by:** Core Peptides, Biotech Peptides, SwissChems, Limitless Biotech, most vendors.
- **Pricing [OBSERVED]:** Broadly competitive; varies by vial size; commonly bundled as blends.
- **COA/testing expectation:** Batch HPLC + MS; blends need component clarity.
- **Risk:** **MEDIUM-HIGH.** Frequently marketed (by others) with muscle/performance/anti-aging claims — that *marketing* is the risk, not the listing itself. **[REQUIRES COUNSEL]**
- **Phase:** Phase 1 *only* with strict research-record copy; **no** performance/muscle/anti-aging language.
- **Safer naming:** "[Compound] — research peptide, [purity]%, [vial size], COA available." **Risky:** "for muscle growth," "anti-aging," "GH boost."

## Category 3 — Cosmetic / copper peptides
- **Examples [OBSERVED]:** GHK-Cu, copper tripeptide-1; (topical-research framed).
- **Sold by:** Core Peptides, Cosmic Peptides, most vendors.
- **Pricing [OBSERVED]:** GHK-Cu 50mg ~$49–59 (Core Peptides).
- **Risk:** **MEDIUM.** Lower than systemic compounds but still avoid skin/anti-aging outcome claims.
- **Phase:** Phase 1. **Safer naming:** "GHK-Cu — copper peptide research compound, [purity]%." **Risky:** "anti-aging," "skin healing," "collagen booster."

## Category 4 — Tissue / cell-signaling research peptides
- **Examples [OBSERVED]:** BPC-157, TB-500 (thymosin beta-4 fragment), BPC/TB blends, KPV.
- **Sold by:** Essentially every vendor; the category "anchors."
- **Pricing [OBSERVED]:** BPC-157 5mg ~$39–49; TB-500 5mg ~$42–52 (Core Peptides).
- **Risk:** **MEDIUM-HIGH** *because of how others market them* (healing/recovery/injury). The compounds are core demand drivers; the claims are the danger. BPC-157 was explicitly named in FDA's Sept 2025 letters context.
- **Phase:** Phase 1 with strict research-record copy. **Safer naming:** technical record only. **Risky:** "healing," "recovery," "injury repair," "gut health."

## Category 5 — Mitochondrial / metabolic research peptides
- **Examples [OBSERVED]:** MOTS-c, SS-31 (elamipretide-class), 5-Amino-1MQ, NAD+-adjacent.
- **Sold by:** Cosmic Peptides, PureRawz, various.
- **Risk:** **MEDIUM-HIGH** if marketed for metabolism/weight/energy outcomes.
- **Phase:** Phase 2 (evaluate per-compound with counsel). **Safer naming:** technical record. **Risky:** "fat loss," "metabolism," "energy," "longevity outcome."

## Category 6 — Cognitive / "nootropic" research peptides
- **Examples [OBSERVED]:** Semax, Selank, Dihexa, Cerebrolysin-class.
- **Sold by:** PureRawz, ChemYo (nootropic lane), various.
- **Risk:** **MEDIUM.** Avoid cognition/mood outcome claims.
- **Phase:** Phase 2. **Safer naming:** technical record. **Risky:** "focus," "memory," "anti-anxiety."

## Category 7 — Custom synthesis & peptide libraries (B2B)
- **Examples [OBSERVED]:** Custom-sequence synthesis, OEM/white-label, bulk supply (Evolve Peptides, PurePeptide Labs, Biosynth at the pharma end).
- **Risk:** **LOWER public-claims risk** (B2B, institutional, documentation-native) but higher operational complexity.
- **Phase:** Phase 5 (researcher/wholesale portal). **[INFERENCE]** Strong long-term differentiator and a genuinely "research-use" credible revenue line.

## Category 8 — Assay / research supplies (NON-injection)
- **Examples [OBSERVED]:** reference standards, lab consumables (NOT bacteriostatic water, NOT syringes, NOT needles).
- **Risk:** **DO NOT** sell injection supplies. **[FACT]** FDA cited bacteriostatic water "sitting in the same cart" as evidence of intended human use. This is a hard exclusion.
- **Phase:** Optional Phase 2, strictly non-injection lab items only.

## Pricing mechanics (how the market prices)

- **[OBSERVED]:** Pricing tiers by vial size (e.g., 2mg/5mg/10mg) and by single-vial vs kits/blends.
- **[OBSERVED]:** Promo cadence: welcome codes (WELCOME15), email-list early access, seasonal sales (15–25% off), bulk discounts, free shipping thresholds (~$200).
- **[OBSERVED]:** Payment-method-based incentives common in the category (crypto/e-check/Zelle discounts) — **[INFERENCE]** a workaround for card-acceptance fragility. We should *not* rely on this; we should secure proper card processing instead.
- **[INFERENCE]:** Premium positioning supports list prices at or modestly above the mid-market *if* COA/documentation quality is visibly superior. Don't compete on being cheapest.

## Apex catalog recommendation (summary)

| Phase | Categories | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | Tissue/signaling (BPC-157, TB-500), GH secretagogues, copper/cosmetic (GHK-Cu) — all as technical records | High demand, anchor SKUs, manageable *if copy is disciplined* |
| **Phase 2** | Mitochondrial/metabolic, cognitive peptides, non-injection lab items | Add breadth after copy/compliance framework proven |
| **Phase 5** | Custom synthesis, libraries, wholesale | Credible research-use revenue; B2B durability |
| **Avoid** | GLP-1-adjacent (semaglutide/tirzepatide/retatrutide), all injection supplies | Highest enforcement + payment risk |
