## A. Model references (operators worth learning from structurally)

### 1. Cosmic Peptides — *documentation-first newer entrant*
- **Overview/positioning [OBSERVED]:** "Buy peptides with confidence / verified purity." Leads with purity (>99%) and independent third-party HPLC + Mass Spec **per batch**.
- **Catalog/pricing [OBSERVED]:** Broad research-peptide catalog; WELCOME15 (15% off) capture; competitive pricing; same-day ship before 2PM CST.
- **Trust/COA presentation [OBSERVED]:** Purity + testing front and center; Trustpilot reviews referenced.
- **Funnel [OBSERVED]:** Shopify-style cart; email capture discount; reviews as social proof.
- **What we can learn:** Documentation-led storefront with a clean, premium, non-bodybuilding aesthetic. This is close to our intended lane.
- **What not to copy:** Don't assume their RUO framing is legally safe; verify every COA is genuinely batch-specific. **[REQUIRES COUNSEL]**
- **Strength 7 / Risk 5.** Sources: cosmicpeptides.com; trustpilot.com.

### 2. Sports Technology Labs — *accredited-lab COA publishing*
- **[OBSERVED]:** Publishes third-party COAs from an accredited US lab; HPLC/MS; min 96% or 99% purity; fast shipping.
- **Learn:** Publishing named-lab COAs (not "available on request") is a strong trust differentiator.
- **Don't copy:** SARM-heavy catalog increases surface area; keep our Phase-1 catalog tighter. **Strength 7 / Risk 5.** Source: sportstechnologylabs.com.

### 3. Lone Star Peptide Co. — *provenance + triple-testing narrative*
- **[OBSERVED]:** "Texas-grade purity," ≥99%, independently tested + triple-verified, same-day Houston shipping. Newer (orders opened ~June 2026).
- **Learn:** Provenance/origin story + testing rigor is a clean premium angle without human-use claims.
- **Strength 6 / Risk 5.** Source: lonestarpeptideco.com.

### 4. Biotech Peptides — *research-lab framing + open card acceptance*
- **[OBSERVED]:** "For scientific research," multiple rounds HPLC + MS; openly advertises "credit cards accepted."
- **Learn:** Confident research framing + clear payment story builds trust. **[INFERENCE]** Open card acceptance implies a high-risk merchant account is in place.
- **Strength 7 / Risk 5.** Sources: biotechpeptides.com; outliyr.com.

### 5. ChemYo — *reputation + third-party testing (SARM lane)*
- **[OBSERVED]:** Delaware-based; all products third-party tested; S&N Labs COAs cited; strong community reputation.
- **Learn:** Consistency + testing transparency builds durable reputation. **Strength 7 / Risk 6.** Source: outliyr.com.

## B. Major incumbents (scale, with caveats)

### 6. Core Peptides
- **[OBSERVED]:** Launched ~2021; 78+ SKUs; cGMP claims; mid-range pricing (BPC-157 5mg ~$39–49); free shipping >$200; payment via cards/Venmo/Zelle/CashApp (per reviews); strong review-site presence.
- **Risk flags [OBSERVED]:** Carries semaglutide and other GLP-1-adjacent SKUs — the highest-enforcement category.
- **Learn:** Catalog breadth, promo cadence, shipping clarity. **Don't copy:** GLP-1 SKUs + peer-to-peer payment reliance (Zelle/CashApp) signal payment fragility. **Strength 7 / Risk 7.** Sources: corepeptides.com; muscleandbrawn.com; peptidepick.com.

### 7. SwissChems
- **[OBSERVED]:** Broad catalog (peptides/SARMs/nootropics/PCT); COAs on listings but sometimes request-only; labs not always named; community reports of repeated COAs across batches; refund-if-fails-test policy reported.
- **[FACT]:** Received an FDA warning letter (Dec 2024).
- **Learn:** Catalog merchandising + test-it-yourself guarantee. **Don't copy:** non-batch-specific COA practices; warning-lettered claims posture. **Strength 6 / Risk 8.** Sources: peptidepick.com; fda.gov.

### 8. Limitless Biotech (formerly Limitless Life Nootropics)
- **[OBSERVED]:** Rebrand; per-batch COA claims; "robust affiliate network" + expert endorsements.
- **Learn:** Affiliate-network-led growth model. **Don't copy:** endorsement-driven claims need tight FTC + human-use guardrails. **Strength 6 / Risk 6.** Source: limitlesslifenootropics.com.

## C. Cautionary cases (study the failure modes)

### 9. Peptide Sciences — *the most important lesson*
- **[FACT]:** One of the most established and relatively compliance-minded peptide-only vendors; **voluntarily shut down March 2026** amid FDA pressure.
- **Lesson [INFERENCE]:** Being the "best" grey-market operator did not confer safety. Survival depends on copy discipline, category selection, payment resilience, and legal posture — not catalog size or reputation. **Strength (historical) 8 / Risk 9.** Sources: peptidesexplorer.com; peptidelaws.com; muscleandbrawn.com.

### 10. Summit Research — *RUO + weight-loss social = enforcement*
- **[FACT]:** FDA warning letter 695607 (12/10/2024). **[OBSERVED]:** Active Facebook marketing for weight loss / metabolic health.
- **Lesson:** Social copy is part of the "totality of marketing." RUO on the site does not cure weight-loss claims on Facebook. **Risk 10.** Sources: fda.gov; trustpilot.com.

### 11. USApeptide.com
- **[FACT]:** FDA warning letter 696885 (02/26/2025). **Lesson:** RUO disclaimers did not prevent enforcement. **Risk 9.** Source: fda.gov.

### 12. Prime Peptides & Xcel Peptides
- **[FACT]:** Named in the Dec 2024 FDA cluster for selling semaglutide/tirzepatide/retatrutide as unapproved drugs; continued operating under pressure. **Lesson:** GLP-1 marketing is the single highest-risk activity. **Risk 9.** Sources: fda.gov; bariatricreports.org.

### 13. Amino Asylum
- **[FACT]:** Reported FDA raid (June 2025). **[OBSERVED]:** Low prices, inconsistent testing, mixed CS reviews. **Lesson:** Cheap + broad + under-tested invites both regulator and customer disputes. **Risk 9.** Sources: muscleandbrawn.com; trustpilot.com.

### 14. GLP-1 Solution
- **[FACT]:** FDA warning letter 715883 (09/09/2025) in the 50+ GLP-1 compounding sweep. **Lesson:** The compounding/telehealth GLP-1 lane is under the heaviest fire. Stay out of it. Sources: fda.gov; wsgr.com.

## Cross-competitor patterns (synthesis)

**[OBSERVED] common strengths to adopt structurally:** (1) batch-specific, named-lab COAs with HPLC + LC-MS; (2) ≥99% purity claims backed by documents; (3) clean datasheet product pages; (4) clear shipping + refund policy; (5) email capture + referral/affiliate program with dashboards.

**[OBSERVED] common failure modes to avoid:** (1) GLP-1/weight-loss copy; (2) RUO disclaimer used as a fig leaf over human-use language; (3) injection-supply cross-sells; (4) peer-to-peer-only payments; (5) vague/recycled COAs and unnamed labs; (6) testimonials implying human outcomes.
