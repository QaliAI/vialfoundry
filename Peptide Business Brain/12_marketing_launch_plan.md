# 12 — Marketing Launch Plan

*Aggressive but disciplined. Every tactic passes the `09` guardrails. No paid ads that violate platform rules; no human-use/medical claims; no deceptive influencer marketing. Labels: [INFERENCE] unless noted.*

## Positioning strategy

**"The documented research peptide catalog."** Compete on verification, transparency, and premium presentation — not price, not outcomes. The brand sounds and looks like an analytical lab-supply company, not a supplement store. Differentiators: batch-specific named-lab COAs (HPLC + LC-MS), provenance/traceability, clean datasheet pages, controlled inquiry/access workflow.

## Audience segments

1. **Prosumer research buyers** — quality- and documentation-sensitive; read review/rating sites.
2. **Independent researchers / small labs** — want COAs, lot traceability, reliable supply.
3. **B2B / institutional (Phase 5)** — custom synthesis, bulk, white-label.
4. **Affiliates / content partners** — documentation-focused reviewers and rating sites.

## Launch channels (priority order)

1. **SEO / content** (primary) — research-context + documentation content (`07`).
2. **Independent rating/COA sites** — submit COAs to Finnrick / Best Peptide Brands for editorially clean listings + backlinks.
3. **Email** — capture + transactional + guardrailed lifecycle.
4. **Curated affiliate/referral** — contractual, claim-controlled (`06`).
5. **Community/B2B** — research-context participation (Reddit under rules, LinkedIn, researcher newsletters).
6. **Creator sponsorships** (cautious) — only documentation-focused creators under strict terms.

## Channels to treat as RISKY / avoid

- Meta/Google paid ads for the category (routine bans; not viable). **[FACT]**
- Weight-loss/bodybuilding influencers and content.
- Any GLP-1/human-outcome messaging anywhere.

## SEO strategy

Execute the `07` 90-day plan: technical foundation → compound datasheets + documentation hub → authority/backlinks. Own research-context and documentation terms; avoid the high-risk weight-loss cluster entirely.

## Affiliate / referral strategy

Launch a **curated** program (not open self-serve): contractual prohibited-claims appendix, FTC disclosure mandate, monitoring, clawback. Start with rating/COA sites and documentation-focused reviewers. Tiered commission (10% base → up to 20% compliant top performers), 30-day cookie, auto-applied codes. See `06` and `13`.

## Email strategy

- Capture via welcome offer (research-context, no outcome claims).
- Transactional confirmations (inquiry/access).
- Guardrailed lifecycle: new-batch COA notices, availability updates, documentation/knowledge content. No medical/outcome copy. Honor CAN-SPAM (clear sender, unsubscribe, physical address).

## LinkedIn / community / B2B strategy

- LinkedIn presence as a credible lab-supply brand (quality standards, documentation explainers, custom-synthesis capability).
- Researcher newsletters/communities; supplier directories.
- Reddit/forums: transparency-first participation under each community's rules; never advertise claims.

## Sponsor / creator strategy (cautious)

Only creators who will use research-context framing, disclose per FTC, and sign claim guardrails. Prefer "how to evaluate a supplier / read a COA" educational angles over product hype. Right to review content + terminate.

## Content calendar (first 90 days — sample cadence)

- **Month 1:** 7 flagship datasheets + 5 trust/documentation pages; 2 knowledge articles/week (COA, HPLC vs LC-MS, supplier-vetting).
- **Month 2:** 3 category hubs; 2 articles/week; COA library live; rating-site outreach.
- **Month 3:** remaining datasheets; 2 articles/week; comparison/evaluation content; first curated affiliates onboarded; 5–10 backlinks.

## Outreach plan

- Processors (parallel quotes) — `13` template 1.
- Rating/review sites (clean listing + backlink) — `13` template 2.
- Creators (cautious, claim-controlled) — `13` template 3.
- Suppliers/labs (documentation requests) — `13` templates 4–5.

## Risk controls

- Sitewide copy guardrails (lint/checklist) on every surface incl. email + affiliate assets.
- Review moderation (no human-outcome testimonials).
- No GLP-1, no injection supplies, no dosing content.
- Attorney sign-off on copy framework + affiliate terms before launch. **[REQUIRES COUNSEL]**

## KPIs

- SEO: indexed pages, ranking keywords (research-context), organic sessions, datasheet/COA views.
- Conversion: inquiry-start → submit rate, access requests, email signups.
- Affiliate: # compliant partners, referred sessions/inquiries, violation rate (target 0).
- Trust: COA views, return/dispute signals, review sentiment.
- Post-payment: orders, AOV, chargeback rate (keep below processor thresholds).

## 30 / 60 / 90-day plan

- **30:** MVP polished + demo; technical SEO live; 7 datasheets + trust pages; analytics events; processor outreach started; copy guardrails enforced.
- **60:** Soft launch; full Phase-1 catalog; COA library; email flows; rating-site outreach; first backlinks; (target) processor shortlisted.
- **90:** Public launch readiness; knowledge hub; curated affiliates onboarded; (target) processor approved → plan checkout integration; review KPIs and double down on rising research-context terms.
