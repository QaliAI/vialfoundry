# 07 — Content / SEO Landscape & Safe Plan

*Labels: [FACT]/[OBSERVED]/[INFERENCE]. SEO is our highest-leverage channel because paid ads are largely unavailable. All keyword/content choices below are filtered through the copy guardrails in `09`.*

## Why SEO is the channel

- **[FACT]:** Most peptide brands cannot run paid ads (routine bans on Meta/Google for this category). Specialized "peptide SEO" agencies exist precisely because organic is the viable lane (Lantern Sol, Peptide SEO, The SEO Clinic, NexaMed).
- **[FACT]:** US peptide search demand ~10.1M/mo (Jan 2026); ~60% weight-loss focused — but that 60% is the highest-risk, highest-competition slice we deliberately avoid.
- **[INFERENCE]:** Our SEO wins by owning the **research-context, compound-record, and documentation** lane that claim-heavy competitors can't safely occupy and pharma B2B suppliers ignore.

## Competitor SEO patterns [OBSERVED]

- Compound-name transactional pages ("Buy [compound]") + "[vendor] review" + "[A] vs [B]" comparisons.
- Buyer-intent title structuring (e.g., "[Compound] | Buy Online | [Brand]").
- Review/affiliate sites dominate the "best vendor" SERPs.
- **Content that got companies in trouble [FACT]:** blog/social copy implying weight loss, glucose/insulin, muscle, healing, GLP-1 comparisons — cited in FDA letters as evidence of human-use intent.

## SEO strategy for Apex (safe lane)

1. **Compound technical pages** as the SEO backbone — datasheet format, not outcome copy.
2. **Documentation hub** — COA methodology, HPLC/LC-MS explainers, batch traceability (genuinely useful, low risk, hard for competitors to match).
3. **Glossary / knowledge hub** — analytical and research terminology.
4. **Trust pages** — testing partners, quality standards, shipping/returns, RUO policy.
5. **Internal linking silos**: Category → compound record → COA/method → glossary.
6. **Schema**: Product, Organization, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage (research-context Qs only), Article. Avoid MedicalEntity/health-claim schema.

## 90-day SEO plan

- **Days 1–30 (foundation):** Technical SEO (site speed, schema, sitemap, internal-link architecture); publish 7 flagship compound records + 5 documentation/trust pages; set GSC + analytics events.
- **Days 31–60 (authority):** Publish 10 documentation/methodology articles + 3 category hubs; build COA library; begin outreach to independent rating sites (Finnrick, Best Peptide Brands) for editorially clean listings/backlinks.
- **Days 61–90 (expansion):** Add 10 more articles + remaining compound records; comparison/evaluation content (supplier-vetting, not vendor-bashing); earn 5–10 quality backlinks; review GSC for rising buyer-intent research terms and double down.

## Backlink opportunities [OBSERVED/INFERENCE]

Independent rating/COA-verification sites (Finnrick, Best Peptide Brands); research/lab procurement resources; glossary/citation links from documentation content; B2B/researcher newsletters; supplier directories. Avoid link schemes and claim-heavy blog networks.
