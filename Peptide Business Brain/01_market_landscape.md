# 01 — Market Landscape

*Scope: U.S.-visible online research-peptide / research-chemical ecommerce ecosystem. Labels: [FACT] / [OBSERVED] / [INFERENCE] / [REQUIRES COUNSEL].*

## Market categories

The online ecosystem clusters into distinct operator types:

1. **Research-peptide sellers (grey market).** Direct-to-consumer storefronts selling vialed peptides labeled "research use only / not for human consumption." Examples: Core Peptides, Cosmic Peptides, Lone Star Peptide Co., Biotech Peptides, Sports Technology Labs. This is the lane our brand targets.
2. **Research-chemical / SARM shops.** Broader catalogs spanning SARMs, nootropics, PCT compounds, and peptides. Examples: SwissChems, Amino Asylum, ChemYo, PureRawz. Higher catalog breadth, generally higher regulatory surface area.
3. **Lab / reagent suppliers (B2B, pharma-grade).** Bachem, CPC Scientific, PolyPeptide Group, Biosynth, Phoenix Pharmaceuticals — GMP API and custom-synthesis suppliers serving pharma and institutional research. **[OBSERVED]** Much stronger compliance posture; serve a different (institutional) buyer. Useful as a credibility/aesthetic reference, not a direct competitor.
4. **Compounding / telehealth-adjacent operators.** Pharmacies and telehealth platforms compounding GLP-1s (semaglutide/tirzepatide). **[FACT]** Heavy FDA + Novo Nordisk enforcement focus in 2025–2026. Different legal regime (compounding/503A/503B). Not our lane.
5. **Affiliate / referral sites.** "Best peptide vendor" ranking sites that monetize via affiliate links and coupons (Outliyr, PeptidePick, Muscle and Brawn, SARMGuide, Finnrick, Best Peptide Brands).
6. **Review / ratings platforms.** Trustpilot pages, Finnrick Analytics (claims independent lab-test-based ratings of 224 suppliers), Best Peptide Brands (COA-verification angle).
7. **Creator / social ecosystem.** YouTube reviewers, Reddit communities (r/Peptides, r/SARMs-adjacent), biohacking newsletters/podcasts, Instagram/TikTok creators. **[OBSERVED]** Heavily human-use-claim oriented — a regulatory minefield for brand partnerships.
8. **SEO / content ecosystem.** Specialized "peptide SEO" agencies (Lantern Sol, Peptide SEO, The SEO Clinic, NexaMed) explicitly market growth-via-organic-search because paid ads are routinely banned.
9. **High-risk payment ecosystem.** Specialized merchant-account brokers and gateways (AllayPay, Corepay, Instabill, Easy Pay Direct, PayBlox, Vector Payments, Durango, PaymentCloud, PayKings).
10. **Supplier / testing / COA ecosystem.** Contract manufacturers, fill/finish, and third-party analytical labs (HPLC/LC-MS) that produce the COAs vendors display.

## What appears to be growing

- **[FACT]** Overall peptide search demand is large and rising (~10.1M/mo US, Jan 2026 per Lantern Sol). GLP-1/weight-loss is the largest slice (~60%).
- **[OBSERVED]** New, cleaner-looking entrants launching in 2025–2026 (Cosmic Peptides, Lone Star Peptide Co.) positioning on purity/testing/transparency rather than bodybuilding aesthetics.
- **[OBSERVED]** Specialized high-risk payment + SEO service layers are maturing — a sign the category is professionalizing around its constraints.

## What appears saturated

- "Best peptide vendor" affiliate review content — dense and competitive.
- Generic BPC-157 / TB-500 / GLP-1 product pages — every vendor has them.
- Bodybuilding-forum-style vendors with near-identical catalogs and aesthetics.

## What looks sketchy

- **[OBSERVED]** Vendors mixing RUO disclaimers with overt weight-loss/muscle/dosing copy and injection-supply cross-sells — the exact pattern FDA has cited.
- Vendors with non-batch-specific or repeated COAs across batches, no named testing lab, no mass-spec data.
- Crypto-/Zelle-/CashApp-only checkout with no refund clarity.

## What looks professionally executed

- **[OBSERVED]** Batch-specific COAs with HPLC + LC-MS displayed; named accredited labs; ≥99% purity claims backed by documents; test-it-yourself refund guarantees; clean datasheet-style product pages; fast/clear shipping; restrained, technical branding.

## Major risks & shutdown patterns

- **[FACT]** FDA warning letters: Summit Research Peptides (Dec 2024), USApeptide.com (Feb 2025); Dec 2024 cluster to Prime Peptides, Xcel Peptides, SwissChems, Summit Research; **50+ letters in Sept 2025** to GLP-1 compounders/manufacturers and RUO peptide sellers.
- **[FACT]** Amino Asylum reported raided by FDA (June 2025). Peptide Sciences voluntarily shut down (March 2026).
- **[FACT]** Mastercard BRAM + Visa scrutiny → payment terminations are a recurring shutdown vector independent of FDA.
- **[INFERENCE]** Two independent kill-switches exist: regulator (FDA/FTC) and payments (card networks/acquirers). A durable brand must survive both.

## The market gap for a premium research-use catalog brand

**[INFERENCE]** Most competitors fall into two buckets: (a) credible-but-clinical B2B pharma suppliers that ignore the prosumer research buyer, or (b) grey-market stores that look amateur and court enforcement. There is a clear open lane for a brand that:

- Looks genuinely premium and scientific (biotech-supplier aesthetic, datasheet product pages).
- Leads with documentation (COA/batch/HPLC/LC-MS) as the product, not the upsell.
- Keeps **all** public copy in the safer research-context lane (no human-use, no dosing, no injection supplies, no GLP-1 marketing).
- Treats payment-processor readiness and a controlled inquiry/access workflow as first-class features.
- Builds SEO/affiliate growth with hard copy guardrails rather than reckless claims.

This is defensible precisely because it is *harder* and most operators won't do it.
