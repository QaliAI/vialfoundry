# 14 — Management Brief

*Audience: client / decision-makers / investors. Professional, direct, not alarmist. Not legal advice.*

## What we found

The online research-peptide market is large and growing fast (~10.1M U.S. searches/month as of Jan 2026), but it is also under the most aggressive FDA enforcement in its history. In 2024–2026 the FDA issued dozens of warning letters (including a 50+ letter sweep in Sept 2025), conducted raids, and watched even the most established, "compliance-minded" vendor (Peptide Sciences) voluntarily shut down in March 2026. Mainstream payment processors (Stripe/PayPal/Square/Shopify) refuse the category, so a specialized high-risk merchant-account ecosystem has formed around it.

## Why this opportunity is real

Demand is enormous and durable. Most competitors look amateurish, make reckless human-use claims, and rely on fragile peer-to-peer payments. The market lacks a genuinely premium, documentation-first brand that occupies the clean "research-use" lane — the space between clinical pharma B2B suppliers and grey-market stores. That gap is ours to take.

## Why it is risky

Two independent kill-switches: (1) **regulatory** — the FDA treats the "research use only" label as meaningless if any site/blog/social/cart copy implies human use; and (2) **payments** — card networks (Mastercard BRAM) terminate non-compliant merchants. The behaviors that make competitors money short-term (weight-loss copy, GLP-1 comparisons, dosing guides, selling injection supplies) are exactly what triggers both. Survival requires permanent copy discipline and careful category selection — not just a good launch.

## What the website MVP solves

The custom Next.js/Supabase platform gives us: a premium, processor-ready storefront; technical (datasheet) product pages; a controlled inquiry/catalog-access workflow with research-use acknowledgement (our conversion event before checkout exists); COA/batch documentation tooling; email automation; analytics readiness; and sitewide copy guardrails. It is built to satisfy both buyer trust and processor underwriting.

## Why payment processing is the main constraint

We can build everything else now, but we cannot sell until an approved high-risk merchant account is signed. Approval depends on a clean, claim-free live site and proper documentation. This is the gating milestone — so we sequence everything toward it and intentionally ship **no live checkout** until it's secured.
