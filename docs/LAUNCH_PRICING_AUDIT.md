## 4. Key Takeaways
1. The reduction of standard shipping from $15.00 to $9.99 eliminates the single largest point of drop-off for small research carts.
2. The repricing of Tesamorelin from $132.00 to $59.99 fixes a catastrophic positioning error that would have completely stalled sales of this SKU.
3. The GLP-1/GIP category (Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, Retatrutide) remains a major revenue driver but carries compressed wholesale pricing in the broader market; owner cost verification is strongly advised before reducing retail prices below margin floors.
