## 2. VIAL & PACKAGING GEOMETRY

- **Peptide / Lyophilized Reference Standards (SKUs 001–009, 011–019)**:
  - **Bottle**: 10mL Type I USP borosilicate neutral clear glass vial.
  - **Stopper**: 20mm chlorobutyl fluoropolymer-coated gray lyophilization stopper.
  - **Cap**: 20mm aluminum crimp collar with anodized cyan/teal flip-off seal.
  - **Content Appearance**: Crisp white/off-white lyophilized solid cake (or deep blue for GHK-Cu, pale crystalline for NAD+).
- **Diluents & Sterile Solvents (SKU 010)**:
  - **Bottle**: 30mL Type I borosilicate sealed molded serum vial.
  - **Cap**: 20mm aluminum crimp collar with cobalt blue flip-off seal.
  - **Content Appearance**: Ultra-pure clear liquid.
- **Bulk Solvents & Mobile Phase (SKU 020)**:
  - **Bottle**: 1000 mL amber glass solvent bottle with amber GL45 screw cap.

## 3. LABEL DESIGN & TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

Every vial label adheres to the standardized Vial Foundry physical label grid:
- **Dimensions**: 50mm × 25mm matte synthetic moisture-resistant stock with satin varnish.
- **Top Accent**: 1.5mm solid Cyan accent band (`#0891B2`).
- **Brand Header**: `VIAL FOUNDRY` wordmark + `RUO` micro-badge.
- **Product Headline**: Clear, bold sans-serif title (Outfit / Inter 18–24pt) displaying the exact active reference standard name.
- **Presentation Subtitle**: Monospace size & formulation tag (e.g. `5 MG · LYOPHILIZED VIAL`).
- **Traceability Block**: Factual CAS Registry Number and SKU Identifier in JetBrains Mono.
- **Legal Compliance Bar**: High-contrast black footer strip with `FOR RESEARCH USE ONLY · NOT FOR HUMAN USE`.
